State-Only Offline Imitation Learning — Masters Thesis
Aug 2025 – Jul 2026

An offline imitation learning framework that learns from sparse, state-only expert demonstrations alongside suboptimal trajectories. Sub-trajectory identification and contrastive likelihood-ratio scoring recover expert-like behavior, with performance comparable to SMODICE and LobsDICE across continuous Gymnasium control domains.

Interactive Steering for Autonomous Driving Handover
Jul 2024 – Jan 2025
Published Research

Level 1–3 driver-assistance systems require a human to safely reclaim control at any moment, making a smooth handover critical for safety. This work introduces an interactive steering module using quasi-direct drive actuation with haptic, audio, and visual feedback, validated through a driving-simulator user study.
